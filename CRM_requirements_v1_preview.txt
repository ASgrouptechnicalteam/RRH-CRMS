2. Business Scope Relevant to CRM 
2.1 RRH 
●​
Commercial properties, agricultural land, open plots and related real-estate categories. 
●​
Rentals are not handled. 
●​
Both company/own inventory and resale/owner-submitted properties are supported. 
●​
Current operating geography is Telangana; the model must support future state expansion. 
2.2 Sonthillu 
●​
Residential/housing properties including apartments/flats, independent houses and villas. 
●​
Rentals are not handled. 
●​
Both company/own inventory and resale/owner-submitted properties are supported. 
●​
Current operating geography is Telangana; the model must support future state expansion. 
2.3 Connected brands 
●​
RRH and Sonthillu are separate companies/brands with separate domains, branding and customer accounts. 
●​
They are connected through the same CRM ecosystem. 
●​
Cross-branding is approved by the owner. 
●​
A property can be associated with both companies, and publication can be independent per brand.
