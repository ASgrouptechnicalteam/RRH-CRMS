2. Executive Summary 
RRH and Sonthillu are two distinct but connected real-estate companies. RRH handles commercial properties, 
agricultural land, open plots and related real-estate opportunities. Sonthillu focuses on residential housing such as 
apartments/flats, independent houses and villas. Both support resale. 
The websites must not be clones. They will share product capabilities and backend integration patterns while 
maintaining separate visual identity, content tone, UX personality and customer accounts. Cross-branding is approved 
but should be subtle and contextual. 
RRH-CRMS remains the authoritative source for property existence, verification, lifecycle and publication eligibility. A 
property may be associated with one or both companies; publication is independently controlled per brand. 
Both websites provide Normal Search and AI Search. AI interprets natural language and multilingual/mixed-language 
input into a structured requirement model. A deterministic matching/ranking engine then retrieves and ranks actual 
CRM-approved inventory. A recommendation layer provides exact, related, nearby, slightly-above-budget and no-result 
recovery options. 
V1 prioritizes practical production features. Advanced ML personalization, complex AI agents and large-scale search 
infrastructure are deferred until actual usage justifies them.

6. Confirmed Business Rules 
1. Rentals are not a website offering. 
2. Sold and Reserved inventory cannot be live/public. 
3. Inventory availability includes Available, Reserved and Sold. 
4. Project lifecycle and inventory availability must be modeled separately. 
5. New vs Resale is an explicit search dimension. 
6. Source type is internal and never public by default. 
7. The same physical property/project may be associated with RRH and Sonthillu. 
8. Publication is independently controlled per company. 
9. Exact property location is not public; approximate location is used. 
10. CRM site verification can capture current latitude, longitude, accuracy, timestamp and employee identity. 
11. Seller identity/contact details are never exposed to customers. 
12. Prices are negotiable; do not present them as falsely fixed rates. 
13. Price ranges may be displayed such as ₹45L – ₹53L. 
14. RERA can be shown as a public verified indicator where applicable; detailed documents remain internal. 
15. Logged-in customers can provide only preferred contact time when identity is already known. 
16. Guests can login or use a guest enquiry form. 
17. Both Request a Call and Call Now are available. 
18. Users can enquire about multiple selected properties in one action. 
19. Homepage primary discovery is Search + AI Search. 
20. Sell Property is a main navbar action, not a dominant homepage hero action. 
21. Website content/UI is English-only. 
22. AI Search supports multilingual and mixed-language input. 
23. RRH and Sonthillu have separate customer accounts/databases. 
24. Core capabilities are shared, but UI/UX must be unique to each brand. 
25. Stock imagery may be used during design where real assets are unavailable.

12.2 Missing vs false vs N/A 
Missing information is not automatically a negative. A plot has no BHK because BHK is not applicable. An apartment 
missing BHK is unknown. This distinction must be explicit in the engine. 
12.3 Ranking 
1. Determine actual requirements.​
2. Identify hard constraints.​
3. Retrieve viable candidates.​
4. Evaluate each applicable requirement.​
5. Ignore N/A fields.​
6. Treat unknown data separately.​
7. Aggregate satisfaction using only the requirements present.​
8. Apply hard-constraint gating.​
9. Apply public/availability/verification rules.​
10. Rank exact matches, then close matches, then alternatives.

The CRM is an application; the public sites are search/discovery products with indexable property/project pages. Next.js 
is therefore proposed for SEO-friendly public pages while retaining React/TypeScript. 
20.2 Why not WordPress 
The product requires typed property APIs, dynamic search, AI interpretation, matching, recommendations, separate 
customer accounts, CRM integration and analytics. A custom TypeScript application is a cleaner fit. 
20.3 AI hosting 
Do not run a large AI model on the Hostinger Business web server. The AI inference layer should be independently 
deployable and accessed through a stable internal interface.

40. Final Product Principle 
The websites should be simple for customers and sophisticated behind the scenes. The user should experience familiar 
real-estate discovery, clear property information, fast Normal Search, useful recommendations and an optional AI 
Search. Complexity should live in the CRM integration, data contracts, matching engine, recommendation layer and 
analytics rather than unnecessary UI. 
RRH and Sonthillu are separate brands. They should look and feel different while providing the same level of product 
quality. Their shared foundation is the connected CRM/API ecosystem, not a shared visual identity. 
This blueprint is the product baseline. The next artifact after review should reconcile it against the actual RRH-CRMS 
repository, database schema, lifecycle and existing API surface, then produce the final implementation-ready 
requirements/data-contract document.
