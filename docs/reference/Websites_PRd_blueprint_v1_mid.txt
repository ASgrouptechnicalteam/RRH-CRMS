6. Confirmed Business Rules 
1. Rentals are not a website offering. 
2. Sold and Reserved inventory cannot be live/public. 
3. Inventory availability includes Available, Reserved and Sold. 
4. Project lifecycle and inventory availability must be modeled separately. 
5. New vs Resale is an explicit search dimension. 
6. Source type is internal and never public by default. 
7. The same physical property/project may be associated with RRH and Sonthillu. 
8. Publication is independently controlled per company. 
9. Exact property location is not public; approximate location is used. 
10. CRM site verification can capture current latitude, longitude, accuracy, timestamp and employee identity. 
11. Seller identity/contact details are never exposed to customers. 
12. Prices are negotiable; do not present them as falsely fixed rates. 
13. Price ranges may be displayed such as ₹45L – ₹53L. 
14. RERA can be shown as a public verified indicator where applicable; detailed documents remain internal. 
15. Logged-in customers can provide only preferred contact time when identity is already known. 
16. Guests can login or use a guest enquiry form.
